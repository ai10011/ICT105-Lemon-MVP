## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class chat group | Students | Save time when items are lost | Click demo link | Clicks/demo views | Students already use chat groups |
| Campus poster QR | Students on campus | Report/search items quickly | Scan QR | QR scans/form visits | Near the problem context |
| Instagram story | Student community | Simple campus utility | Visit landing page | Story clicks | Familiar student channel |

## Launch Sequence
1. Prepare landing page and demo link.
2. Share to selected early-user channel.
3. Track response using spreadsheet, form, or prototype logs.
4. Collect feedback.
5. Revise message, CTA, or prototype feature if needed.
