# Demo Script

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate that LostLink enables students and campus staff to report, search, and track lost or found items through a simple and centralized workflow.

## Presenter Roles

| Member | Role in Demo | Part Presented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Introduction | Homepage, project overview, and problem statement |
| Member | User Demonstration | Submit a lost/found item, search records, and view item details |
| Member | Admin Demonstration | Update item status, dashboard, analytics, and conclusion |

## Demo Flow

| Step | Screen / Feature | What Presenter Says | Requirement ID | Expected User Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Landing/Homepage | Welcome to LostLink. This system helps students report and find lost items on campus through one centralized platform. | FR-01 | Understand the purpose of the system |
| 2 | Main User Pathway | Users can choose to report a lost item, report a found item, or search existing records. | FR-02 | Easy navigation and clear workflow |
| 3 | Input Form | Users enter the item details, category, location, and upload an image before submitting the report. | FR-03, FR-10, FR-11 | Quick and easy reporting |
| 4 | Record List | Users can browse all reports and use search or filters to find relevant items. | FR-05, FR-06 | Faster item searching |
| 5 | Detail View | Users can open a report to view complete item information and status. | FR-07 | Better understanding of reported items |
| 6 | Status/Admin | Campus staff update the report status from Open to Claimed or Resolved. | FR-08, FR-09 | Accurate tracking of item progress |
| 7 | Dashboard/Metrics | The dashboard displays a summary of lost, found, and resolved items. | FR-12 | Quick overview of system activity |
| 8 | Closing | LostLink simplifies the lost-and-found process by providing one organized platform for the campus community. | FR-16 | Understand the value of the system |

## Closing Statement

LostLink provides a simple and centralized way for students and campus staff to report, search, and track lost or found items. By replacing scattered communication channels with one organized platform, it saves time, improves communication, and increases the chances of returning lost belongings.
