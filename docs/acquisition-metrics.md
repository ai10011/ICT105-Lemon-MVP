# Acquisition Metrics — Lemon (Campus Lost & Found MVP)

## Purpose
Define how the group will measure early user interest, landing page engagement, and prototype validation for **Lemon**, a campus-focused digital lost-and-found platform.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Total unique and repeat visits to the project landing page | Total pageview hits logged | Google Analytics / Vercel Web Analytics | At least 250 total pageviews |
| M-02 | CTA Click Rate | Percentage of visitors moving from the landing page to the app prototype or report flow | (CTA Clicks / Landing Page Views) * 100 | Click event triggers on "Report Item" or "Browse Lost Items" buttons | Target: ≥ 35% click rate |
| M-03 | Demo & Prototype Interactions | Total number of users who launched the Figma interactive prototype or tested the reporting form | Count of unique sessions on prototype link / Form entries opened | Figma analytics / Google Form access log | At least 60 active demo attempts |
| M-04 | User Feedback Submissions | Volume of structured user feedback received regarding usability, feature requests, and trust factors | Total submitted feedback forms / Survey responses | Google Forms / Feedback Modal | At least 20 detailed feedback responses |
| M-05 | Signups & Early Testers | Conversion rate of landing page visitors who signed up to become beta testers or registered an item | (Signups or Testers / Landing Page Views) * 100 | User registration database / Beta signup sheet | Target: ≥ 18% overall conversion |

---

## Detailed Metric Analysis & Action Matrix

### Interpretation Strategy: What will the group do if metrics fall short?

| Low Metric | Potential Root Cause | Group Contingency Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M-01: Landing Page Views** | Low distribution or weak channel reach | Distribute QR codes directly on high-density campus bulletin boards (Library, Cafeteria, Student Union). Partner with student council chat groups to share the link. |
| **M-02: CTA Click Rate** | Ambiguous value proposition or weak call-to-action design | Test high-contrast CTA buttons. Rewrite CTA text from *"Learn More"* to explicit actions like *"Report a Lost Item Now"* or *"Find My Item"*. |
| **M-03: Demo Attempts** | High friction or confusing prototype entry point | Remove any login barriers for initial demo access. Add an embedded video preview or dynamic GIF showing how easy item reporting is. |
| **M-04: Feedback Responses** | Form is too long or time-consuming | Reduce feedback form to 3 quick questions (1 rating + 2 short open text fields). Add a quick rating widget inside the app flow. |
| **M-05: Signups / Conversion** | Lack of user trust in safety/privacy of lost item claims | Highlight safety features on the landing page (e.g., student ID verification, secure handover checkpoints on campus). |
